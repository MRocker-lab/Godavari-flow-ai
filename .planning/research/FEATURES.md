# Feature Inventory & Domain Modeling — GODAVARI FLOW AI

## Canonical Rajamahendravaram & Kovvur Dataset

Both portals share a single rich, realistic spatial dataset covering:
1. **Ghats & Cultural Destinations (8):**
   - Pushkar Ghat (Primary VIP & Main Ritual Ghat)
   - Kotilingala Ghat (High-capacity historic ghat)
   - Gautami Ghat (Family & cultural exhibition ghat)
   - Saraswati Ghat (Senior-friendly ramped ghat)
   - Padmavathi Ghat (Central riverfront ghat)
   - Kovvur Gospada Ghat (West bank main ghat)
   - Dowleswaram Barrage Ghat & Sir Arthur Cotton Museum
   - ISKCON Godavari Riverfront Temple
2. **Transport Hubs & Parking Zones (8):**
   - Morampudi Junction Mega Satellite Parking (NH-16 East)
   - Lalacheruvu Outer Ring Parking Hub (North)
   - Arts College Ground Central Parking (Permit/Accessibility Priority)
   - Godavari Railway Station Parking & Transit Point
   - Rajahmundry Main Railway Station Mobility Hub
   - Gokavaram RTC Bus Stand Hub
   - Kovvur Toll Plaza West Bank Parking Hub
   - Dowleswaram Approach Parking Zone
3. **Shuttle Corridors (6):**
   - S1: Morampudi Parking ↔ Pushkar Ghat Express
   - S2: Lalacheruvu Hub ↔ Kotilingala & Gautami Ghats
   - S3: Main Railway Station ↔ Saraswati & Padmavathi Ghats (Wheelchair & Senior Priority)
   - S4: Gokavaram Bus Complex ↔ Riverfront Circular Shuttle
   - S5: Kovvur Parking Hub ↔ Gospada Ghat & Road-cum-Rail Bridge
   - S6: Dowleswaram Barrage ↔ ISKCON & Gautami Ghat
4. **Road & Bridge Segments (10):**
   - Godavari Road-cum-Rail Bridge, 4th Godavari Bridge (Diwan Cheruvu–Kovvur), Pushkar Ghat Approach Road, Main Road / Fort Gate Corridor, Tadithota–Kotilingala Road, JN Road Corridor, Stadium Road, Dowleswaram Bypass, Kovvur Gospada Approach, NH-16 Morampudi Flyover.

## Cross-Portal Impact Matrix

| Authority Action in Command Centre | Immediate Effect in Pilgrim Portal |
|------------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Approve AI Traffic Diversion / Close Road Segment | Journey Planner recalculates routes avoiding closed road; Route Map shows closure badge; Homepage Mobility Status updates |
| Update Parking Lot Occupancy / Status (e.g., Full) | Parking Finder marks lot as Full/Congested and promotes next best satellite lot + linked shuttle |
| Dispatch Extra Shuttles / Change Route Frequency | Shuttle Tracker updates ETA, available capacity, and Journey Planner wait times |
| Create or Escalate Critical Incident at a Ghat | Ghat Destination detail & Journey Planner display active safety advisory banner; AI Concierge warns visitors |
| Apply Digital Twin Intervention to Operational State | Optional "Apply Simulation to Live Demo State" or isolated sandbox comparison mode clearly badged in UI |
